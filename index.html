How it works

One email. Four steps. Nothing hidden.

1Sort

Read and label in a second

Category, priority, mood and language are set for every email. Newsletters and automatic notifications are left alone, so they cost nothing.

2Check the facts

Look it up. Never guess.

The agent finds the order, licence or account in your connected systems and searches your knowledge base before a single word is written.

3Decide

Your rules make the call

Answer, ask only for what is missing, acknowledge, or hand the case to a colleague. Templates and rules decide, not a hunch.

4Reply or hand over

Sent in your words, with a way to stop it

Autopilot shows the reply for a short review time, then sends it. "What the agent checked" shows exactly why it looks the way it does.
